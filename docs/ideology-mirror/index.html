Ideology Mirror

LLMs reflect their creators' ideology. Measure the reflection: red-team the values, gate the drift, steer to your constitution.

1The problem

Every aligned model ships its creator's values — RLHF/DPO inject annotator and corporate preferences, and the creator effect overrides pre-training data. Enterprises deploying base models as-is import an ideology: product voice becomes the vendor's voice, global rollouts hit cultural friction, B2B deployments inherit compliance mismatches.

2The current gap

Model governance covers security, privacy, and hallucinations — not value alignment. Nobody red-teams how a model frames what it says, nobody scores vendors on ideological risk at procurement, and nobody gates CI on alignment drift when the vendor ships a new version.

3How the solution addresses it

A governance measurement framework (simulated stand-ins with parameterized creator ideology; the contribution is the methodology): value profiles across 6 dimensions, a red-team probe battery (framing bias, refusal asymmetry, emphasis divergence), the creator-effect harness (same knowledge, different ideologies → divergence), an AI Constitution with conformance scoring, vendor risk grades (A–F + contract clauses), and a worst-dimension drift gate for CI/CD.

Blueprint of the Ideology Mirror architecture. See the repo for the full implementation.

6Insights

  • Refusal is ideology, not safety alone. Asymmetric refusal patterns are measurable and belong in the risk report.
  • Gate on the worst dimension. Mean drift hides the one axis that moved; the CI gate fails on max per-dimension drift.
  • Steering works when it's explicit. A written constitution plus grounding moves conformance to 1.000 — 'be neutral' is not a constitution.
