Leadership Insights

Join Jira, GitHub, ServiceNow, Workday, and Planview — and answer the questions no single system can.

1The problem

Leadership decisions run on siloed dashboards: engineering sees tickets, finance sees licenses, HR sees attrition. The questions that matter — is the review bottleneck raising change-failure rates? which licenses are actually waste? is the escalation cluster a staffing problem? — require joins nobody performs.

2The current gap

Per-source analytics can't do cross-source inference. Entity resolution across five systems (same person, different IDs) is the unglamorous prerequisite nobody builds, so 'integrated insights' stay slideware.

3How the solution addresses it

An integration layer (entity resolution F1 1.00 over 2,153 rows, with collision and missing-email fallbacks) feeding four leadership lenses — CTO (DORA, PR analytics, bottleneck detector), CFO (license utilization, dollarized reclaim list), Client (CSAT drivers, escalation predictor), HR (productivity/satisfaction, retention risk) — plus a cross-source insight engine where every insight declares its required sources, making the siloed-vs-integrated ablation exact.

6Insights

  • The join is the insight. 6 of 7 findings need ≥2 sources; per-source dashboards can never produce them.
  • A testing engine beats a confirming engine. Reporting 'no link found' on a plausible hypothesis is the feature, not a failure.
  • Entity resolution is the moat. F1 1.00 with collision handling is what makes the rest possible.
